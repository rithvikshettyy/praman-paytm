The problem

A claim is usually cut for reasons visible before filing.

STOPPED

Waiting period

The treatment is still inside the wait. The policy is too young.

STOPPED

Excluded

The procedure is on the policy's list of things it never pays for.

CUT

Room above the cap

The room costs more than the limit, so charges are cut in proportion.

STOPPED

Missing papers

A discharge summary, a bill, an ID, still not in the file.

The problem shows up after the claim is paid for and filed.Nobody warned them in advance

What Praman does

Three things, each one tested.

1

Stops avoidable rejections

A rule engine checks the claim against the policy and bill before filing. One of three answers: file, do not file yet, or file with a known deduction.

2

Sends it to the right party

A router names who owes the answer: insurer, lender or distributor. Each has its own first step and its own clock.

3

Shows the distributor what needs it

A console lists the cases that need the distributor and the complaints waiting for a person, read from recorded events only.

What it does not do: rank or recommend a policypredict approvalscore creditfile anything automatically
How a customer starts

Pick a start. Praman does the rest.

1

Find a policy

Tap-to-answer questions: who is covered (parents too), ages, any illness, cover or payout wanted, years. Then options found online, unranked, and what to check before buying.

2

Check my policy

Send the PDF or a photo and ask anything. Each answer comes from the document or the rules, with its page. If it is not there, Praman says so.

3

Complaint

Say what went wrong, send the policy or just its number. If Praman cannot settle it, or she asks for a person, the complaint is registered with how to reach her.

The verdict

Three outcomes, not two.

OUTCOME 1

File

No rule blocks the claim. Nothing is cut. Go ahead and file.

A clear claimNothing to fix

Do not file yet

After she approves

Praman decides what and to whom. n8n decides how and when.

1

Approve, and agree

She approves the letter and agrees to Praman contacting the insurer for her. Without both, nothing leaves. Until then the screen says "Approved and ready to send".

2

n8n delivers

The letter goes out with account, Aadhaar, PAN and policy numbers masked, signed with a shared secret. n8n sends it and reports back. Only then is it marked sent.

3

n8n watches the clock

It waits out that step's window, then asks Praman what is due. The case's own ladder answers: escalate, ask an agent, wait or stop. The agent gets an email, she gets a nudge.

Honest wording: "sent" means n8n reported it went outnever "the insurer received it"stops if resolved, deleted or consent withdrawn
