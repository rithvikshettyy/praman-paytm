# Example Health Shield: policy wording (example)

1. Definitions
1.1 Sum insured means the most the insurer will pay for all claims in a policy year, as shown in the policy schedule.
1.2 Continuous cover means cover renewed without a break, including cover carried over through portability.

2. Room rent and ICU
2.1 Room rent, boarding and nursing are payable up to 1% of the sum insured per day.
2.2 ICU charges are payable up to 2% of the sum insured per day.
2.3 If the room chosen costs more than this limit, associated medical expenses are reduced in the same proportion. Pharmacy, consumables, implants, medical devices and diagnostics are not reduced.

<!-- page 2 -->

3. Waiting periods
3.1 Initial waiting period: no claim is payable for an illness that starts in the first 30 days of the first policy, except for accidents.
3.2 Specified diseases and procedures, including cataract, hernia, joint replacement and kidney stones, are covered after 24 months of continuous cover.
3.3 Pre-existing diseases are covered after 36 months of continuous cover.

4. Co-payment
4.1 An insured person aged 61 or above at entry bears 10% of every admissible claim.

<!-- page 3 -->

5. Permanent exclusions
5.1 Cosmetic or plastic surgery, unless needed to treat an accident or burns.
5.2 Dental treatment, unless caused by an accident and needing hospitalisation.
5.3 Infertility and assisted reproduction treatment.

6. Claims
6.1 Tell the insurer within 24 hours of an emergency admission, and at least 48 hours before a planned admission.
6.2 For reimbursement, send the claim form and documents within 30 days of discharge.
